Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Turnitout Pro ("we", "our", or "us", powered by the TextHumanizer core engine) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and disclose information when you visit our website, use our AI text transformation engine, or interact with our services.

2. Document & Text Processing Privacy

Your uploaded text and documents (.docx, .pdf, .txt) are processed in memory strictly for the purpose of removing AI markers, rewording text, and calculating uniqueness scores. We do not sell, store permanently, or train public AI models on your private intellectual property.

3. Advertising Partners & Google AdSense

We use third-party advertising companies, primarily Google AdSense and related advertising networks, to serve advertisements when you visit our website.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Cookies & Web Beacons

Like many websites, TextHumanizer Pro uses cookies to store visitor preferences, record user-specific information on pages accessed, and customize content based on browser type. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options.

5. Log Files & Analytics

We maintain standard server log files. The information inside log files includes Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. This information is used solely to analyze trends, administer the site, and ensure server security.

6. GDPR & CCPA Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, you have certain data protection rights, including the right to request access, rectification, erasure, or restriction of your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our advertising practices, please contact us via our official support channels.
